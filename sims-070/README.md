**Job 1 (results/study1_summary.csv).** Observed power 0.05, 0.415, 0.675, 0.91 at planned 0, 0.5, 0.8, 0.95. Among significant results: sensitive 0.50, 0.31, 0.19, 0.10; fragile 0 in every scenario (under the 0.7.x interval rule nothing left the filed interval in 800 trials; outside_ci_any = 0 everywhere). Median largest shift in filed SEs 0.36, 0.35, 0.36, 0.36. One deletion lost significance in 71%, 33%, 38%, 31% of significant results (frag_k1_given_sig).

**Job 2 label changes (results/reclass_changes.csv).** Study 2: 91 of 750 trials change label, all fragile to robust, all non-significant; no significant trial changes. Study 3 (all families): 140 of 1,200 change, all fragile to robust, all non-significant; no significant trial changes. This is the expected effect of dropping the reversed-direction rule: estimates near zero that reversed inside the interval.

**Design grid.** validation/design/design_A_trials.csv has no outside_ci column at 0.6.0, so the grid is not reclassified. Its robustness power is P(significant and robust) and is unaffected by the reversal rule; Table 5 of the plan stays on the 0.6.0 run and Figure 5 is redrawn from validation/design/design_A.csv.

**Duloxetine filed-analysis check: passed** (estimate -2.872048, SE 1.102845, p 0.01011878, 172 participants, 608 observations; logs/job3_public_trials.log). Standard stress test at 0.7.2 (results/public_trials_summary.csv): 23 counted alternatives and 171 deletions, none crossed 0.05, none left the interval, none reversed; largest shift 0.59 filed SEs (jump to reference, p = 0.044); tipping point 1.43 points on the delta grid; five greedy deletions lose significance; verdict robust, not marginal. Note for the draft: PAPER_DRAFT 0.3 reported one crossing from jump to reference (p = 0.053) and the verdict sensitive at 0.6.0; at 0.7.x the imputations use common random numbers across methods (NEWS 0.7.0) and the J2R refit now has p = 0.044, so the verdict is robust. The 0.6.0 numbers are in validation/antidepressant/.

**Didanosine versus zalcitabine (standard stress test, aids).** Filed 0.313 (SE 0.404), p = 0.439, 406 participants, 938 observations. 26 counted alternatives and 406 deletions; none crossed 0.05; one counted sensitivity row, the complete-case MMRM of the estimator family, gives -0.699 (SE 1.45), which is outside the filed interval and on the other side of zero (shift 2.5 filed SEs); tipping point -1.77; verdict fragile under the 0.7.x rule. stress_direction(): 1 of 26 re-analyses reverses the direction (that row); the ranked deletion search reverses the direction after 4 removals (results/aids/aids_direction.rds). This differs from PAPER_DRAFT 0.3 section 5.2, which used the light family set without the estimator family and found no row outside the interval; the complete-case estimator is the difference and it is recorded here as found.

**Job 4.** Figure 2 trials (results/fig02_trials_chosen.csv): power 0 seed 1005 (filed estimate -1.41, p 0.524), power 0.5 seed 2006 (estimate 5.13, p 0.0117), power 0.95 seed 4137 (estimate 7.05, p 0.00072), all robust under the standard plan. Selection rule: the trial whose filed p is closest to the median filed p of its scenario (among significant trials for power 0.5 and 0.95, among all trials for power 0). With an even number of trials (200 and 182 here) the median is the midpoint of the two central p-values, so two trials are always equally close and the original which.min() broke the tie by floating-point noise, which differs between hosts: the server run picked seeds 1113 and 4137, the first laptop run 1005 and 4178. The script now takes every trial within 1e-9 of the smallest distance and keeps the lower seed; applied to either host's Study 1 rows it gives 1005, 2006, 4137. The displayed trials are therefore a different pair at power 0 and the same at power 0.95 as in the server figure. Broken trials (results/broken_trials_chosen.csv): outlier at +10 residual SD, delta 3, seed 27, filed p 0.0294; effect carried by one site, delta 40, seed 30, filed p 0.0285. Their swarms are figures/figS1_broken_trials.* and the leave-one-site-out rows results/broken_trials_site_rows.csv; displays only, no detection rates.

**Figures.** All five rendered and looked at under 0.7.2. Figures 2 and S1 use the multi-row swarm with the legend now reading statistical decision varied, data-handling decision varied, one unit removed; Figure 4 is the duloxetine swarm (two-row legend) and p-strip; Figure 3 the verdict shares and median shift against planned power with the Study 2 designs as small multiples; Figure 5 the design grid.

**Failures.** None. Every job returned status 0 in both runs.
